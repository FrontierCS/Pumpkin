Answer a client on the standard input and output.

Each job that `serve` starts belongs to the member that the link acts for. `serve` refuses to cancel
another member's job unless the client asks for any member's.

Usage: pumpkin controller serve [OPTIONS]

Options:
      --member <member>
          The member that the controller's forced command names for the link (default: none)

  -h, --help
          Print help
